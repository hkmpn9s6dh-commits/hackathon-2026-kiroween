# Product

**Necropolis** is a web app that turns a developer's abandoned GitHub repositories into a walkable graveyard, gives each one an autopsy, and hands back a concrete resurrection plan.

## Who it is for

Developers with 20–200 public repositories, most of them dead. They feel guilty about the pile but have no way to decide what to do with it.

## The job it does

Turn a vague feeling ("I should do something about my old projects") into a decision per project: **revive**, **harvest one module**, or **bury it with a README**.

## Non-negotiables

- Works with only public data. No OAuth, no private repos, no server.
- The demo graveyard must work offline, with zero network requests.
- Every screen must be usable with a keyboard and readable at 16 px body text.
- Halloween is the setting, not the product: every spooky element must make the data easier to read.

## The one memory point

The graveyard is made of your own repositories, and the moon is the only light.
