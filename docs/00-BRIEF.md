# 00 — Brief: Kiroween 2026 (verified 2026-11-12)

## The event

- **Hackathon**: Kiroween 2026 — AWS Kiro's annual Halloween hackathon, hosted on Devpost.
- **Page**: https://kiroween.devpost.com/ · **Rules**: https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules
- **Submission period**: 30 Oct 2026 09:00 PT → **4 Dec 2026 14:00 PT** (= 5 Dec 2026 06:00 Beijing).
- **Judging**: 16 Dec 2026 → 29 Jan 2027. Winners around 3 Feb 2027.
- **Prize pool**: $100,000+. Overall 1st $30,000 / 2nd $20,000 / 3rd $10,000. Each category $5,000. Bonuses: Startup $10,000, Ghost of Kiroween Past $2,000, Kiro Crew App $1,000, Student Pro Max ×4, Best Demo $1,000, Blog 2,000 credits ×100, Social Blitz 3-month Pro+ ×5.
- **Scale**: 12,496 participants on the page when checked (2026-11-12).

## Eligibility (checked against the official rules)

- Individuals at or above the age of majority where they reside; teams up to 3; organisations.
- Excluded countries/regions: Quebec, Argentina, Australia, Brazil, Italy, Philippines, Russia, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Belarus, Crimea, the so-called DNR/LNR, the UAE, and any OFAC-comprehensively-sanctioned country. **Mainland China is not on the list.**
- No entry fee, no travel, no real-name verification.

## Hard requirements

1. **Kiro is mandatory.** The project must be built using Kiro (IDE, CLI, Web and/or Kiro Crew). Stage One is a pass/fail check of theme + Kiro use; insufficient Kiro use can disqualify.
2. **Public GitHub repo** (not GitLab/BitBucket):
   - all source, assets and run instructions;
   - an OSI licence visible in the About section;
   - **`/.kiro` at the repo root** (specs, hooks, steering instruction files). Must not be gitignored.
3. **A working app URL**, free and available to judges through the judging period.
4. **Demo video under 3 minutes**, public on YouTube, showing the project running on its target device. No third-party trademarks or copyrighted music.
5. **Text description** of features and value.
6. **A write-up of how Kiro was used** — which surface (IDE/CLI/Web/Crew), custom agents, hooks, spec-driven development, steering, MCP, PBT, Kiro powers.
7. All materials in **English**.
8. New project: created after 30 Oct 2026 09:00 PT. Starter templates and libraries are allowed; any other pre-existing code must be disclosed.

## Categories (choose one)

- **Resurrection** — revive a dead technology.
- **Frankenstein** — stitch incompatible technologies into one app.
- **Skeleton Crew** — a lean template plus two distinct apps (repo needs two app folders).
- **Costume Contest** — any app, but the interface must be haunting, polished and unforgettable. *← our category*

## Judging (Stage Two, five equally weighted criteria)

| Criterion | Weight | What judges look for |
|---|---|---|
| Potential Impact | 20% | A credible case for a real problem and a real audience; useful, intuitive, accessible; would someone actually use it? |
| Technological Implementation | 20% | How thoroughly and skillfully Kiro is used; a thoughtful AI-development strategy; variety of Kiro tools; depth of understanding. |
| Design | 20% | A complete, coherent product experience, not a technical proof of concept; intuitive, polished, intentional. |
| Innovation / Idea | 20% | Creative and novel; differs from existing concepts; unusual technologies or public resources combined uniquely. |
| Presentation | 20% | The video demonstrates the project end to end; the pitch conveys problem, user and why it matters; easy to follow, memorable. |
